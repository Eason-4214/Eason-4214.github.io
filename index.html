About

I am a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My research focuses on human–computer interaction, human factors, and autonomous driving.

I study how people perceive, evaluate, and interact with intelligent systems. My work spans driver situation awareness, human–AI judgment, and AI-assisted user experience design, combining behavioral experiments, usability evaluation, and eye-tracking.

Before joining PolyU, I received my master’s degree in Information and Interaction Design from South China University of Technology and my bachelor’s degree in Industrial Design from Sichuan University.
